Game Summary

Roguelike wave-survival game. Survive 15 minutes against escalating waves. Defeat enemies for XP, level up to choose upgrades across 5 weapon types: Wand, Spin, Chain, Orbit, and Bomb. Collect power pickups like Fire Cone, Speed, and Shield to turn the tide.

Hotkeys

  • WASD / Arrow Keys — Move
  • Shift — Dash (3s cooldown, grants invincibility)
  • 1 2 3 — Quick-select level-up cards
  • Enter / Space — Confirm selection
  • ESC — Pause
  • Ctrl+T — Text test scene
  • Ctrl+M — Audio test scene

Enemy Types

  • Grunt — Standard enemy, moderate speed/health. Flanks and circles the player.
  • Dasher — Fast, telegraphed charge attack with red indicator. Spawns after 30s.
  • Brute — Slow but tanky (12 HP), high damage. Enrages when damaged. Steals health/power pickups. Spawns after 90s.
